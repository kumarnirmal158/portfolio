About Me

Engineering Discipline Meets Applied Artificial Intelligence

I am an analytical engineer transitioning into Data Science, Machine Learning, and Generative AI, bringing structured systems thinking and operational rigor to modern AI engineering. I hold an Advanced Professional Certification in Data Science from HCL GUVI / IITM Pravartak and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from JCT College of Engineering and Technology (2014).

Most recently, I worked as an AI/ML Intern on the CET Team at Siam Computing (Sept 2026 – Nov 2026), architecting end-to-end multimodal and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines.
